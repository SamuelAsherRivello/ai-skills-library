# Research dependencies

Every blocking investigation needs a dedicated research proposal created or revised through the planning workflows. Do not hide it inside an implementation task. Record its question, owner, evidence deliverable, dependent proposal IDs, completion criteria, decision approver and exact unblock conditions in the program.

Reuse an existing proposal when it answers the same question. Combine related questions only when they share an investigation and deliverable; do not group unrelated research merely to reduce thread count. Research may itself have prerequisite research, but the dependency graph must remain acyclic.

Front-load ready research across every dependency chain, concurrently where ownership permits. Keep dependent implementation provisional and blocked until the evidence exists. Independent implementation need not wait for unrelated research unless the user set that gate.

On research completion, review evidence and obtain required decisions for each consumer. Update readiness separately for each dependent; one investigation may unblock some consumers but not others. An inconclusive result is still evidence, not permission to invent an answer or waive acceptance criteria. Record remaining questions and retain affected blocks.

New research discovered during execution becomes another proposal and dependency edge. Pause affected implementation, preserve its work, and continue independent authorized work. Respect planning approval boundaries and explicit user gates; ask about circular all-plans-before-research gates rather than bypass them.
